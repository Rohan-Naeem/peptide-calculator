Privacy Policy

Information about privacy, data handling and website usage.

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how PeptideCalc handles information associated with your use of this website, including its calculator, informational pages and related website services.

Information We Collect

PeptideCalc does not require users to create an account simply to access or use the calculator. The website is designed to provide calculator and educational resources without requiring users to submit personal information.

Calculator Information

Values entered into the calculator are designed to be processed within the user's browser to produce mathematical results. PeptideCalc does not intentionally require users to submit personal information in order to perform calculations.

Users should avoid entering unnecessary personal, confidential or sensitive information into any website fields.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

This website may use cookies or similar technologies when required for website functionality, analytics, security, advertising or other services. The use of these technologies may vary depending on the features and services implemented on the website.

Analytics and Usage Information

If analytics services are used, they may collect general information about website usage, such as pages visited, approximate usage patterns, device information or browser information. This information may be used to understand website performance and improve the user experience.

Third-Party Services

PeptideCalc may use third-party services for purposes such as website hosting, analytics, advertising, security or embedded content. These third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Data Security

Reasonable measures may be used to help protect information associated with the website. However, no method of transmitting or storing information online can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children's Privacy

PeptideCalc is not intended to encourage children to provide personal information. Users should not submit unnecessary personal or sensitive information through the website.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the website, its features, third-party services or applicable requirements change. Any updated version will be published on this page.

Contact

For privacy-related questions or concerns, use the contact information provided on the website.
