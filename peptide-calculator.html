How to Calculate Peptide Dosage

This peptide calculator uses mathematical relationships between peptide quantity, volume and concentration to produce the requested result. These relationships are commonly used in peptide dosage and reconstitution calculations. Follow the four steps below, in the same order as the calculator fields above.

01

Step 1: Determine Peptide Amount

Enter the strength of the peptide in the vial, in mg. Use a quick-pick value (2, 5, 10 or 15 mg) or type your own amount.

02

Step 2: Add Water Volume

Enter the diluent (water) volume in mL, for example 1, 2, 3 or 5 mL. Together with the peptide amount, this sets the concentration for the calculation.

03

Step 3: Set Your Dose

Enter the dose of peptide you want to calculate, in mcg — for example 100, 250, 500 or 1,000 mcg.

04

Step 4: Check Your Result

Click Calculate and read the result in the Results box. It shows the concentration and the required volume for the dose you entered.

A peptide reconstitution calculator can help organize mathematical values related to quantity, volume and concentration. Always verify calculations independently when accuracy is important and consult an appropriately qualified professional for medical decisions.
